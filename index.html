How it works

One daily fifteen, in three parts

1

Warm-up

Two minutes on the building block today’s topic rests on, so the gaps get fixed first.

2

Lesson and questions

One step per screen. Each step shows what to do, why it’s needed and what it builds on, with “I don’t understand” always there.

3

Teach it back

Explain it in your own words, on paper or out loud. If you can teach it, you know it.

Stuck? From stuck to fixed in about two minutes.

1 · Point

Tap the first step that stops making sense.

2 · Why or how?

A missing idea gets a picture and a real example.

3 · Check down

One quick question per building block until one comes back right.

4 · Fix

A short lesson and three practice questions on that block.

5 · Climb back

Rung by rung back to the original question, solved.
